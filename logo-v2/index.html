What changed from LogoNew.svg

  • Same drawing, simpler file. The Affinity export nested the shield inside rotated and skewed groups. Every path is now in plain coordinates with the shield's centre line at x = 0, which is what makes the left/right split possible.
  • The windows are now real shapes. In the original, the colour showed wherever the eagle path happened not to cover it, and a clip trimmed the result to the bezel. The clip and the bezel did not quite agree, which is where the border colour bled through. Here the sun and fire areas are their own closed shapes, painted on top of a solid dark shield: rim, shield, eagle panel, windows, each fully covering the one below.
  • The wing joins. Where a wing meets the bezel (the two top corners of the sun and the outer ends of the fire), the corner is replaced by a single curve that leaves the wing and arrives on the bezel without a break in direction. The small joins at the crest and tail feathers get a tight radius so they stay crisp.
  • The tail feathers. In the drawing the two feathers under the body ran on beneath the bezel, so the opening cut them off as blunt, notched stubs that did not match left to right. Crisp, Soft, Inset and Framed now use one tapered feather each side, mirrored, ending in a point clear of the bezel.
  • Full eagle, zoomed. The refined eagle from the first shield page at 1.45 times the shield's width, the proportion used there, cropped by the bezel. It has one open field instead of two windows, so the fire colour fades in under the sun colour.
  • Diagonal bands. From LogoNewDiagonal.svg: the three bands and their gradient are flattened into the same coordinates and clipped to the colour windows, so they sit behind the eagle in every cut. The band colours follow the Ember, Flame and Sun swatches.
  • Removed. The hidden red fill, the 0.25 px hairline stroke on the eagle, and hairline gaps under one unit wide.
  • Added. Stars in the night sky of the counterchanged variant, and two one-colour versions.
  • Wordmark. Untouched: the outlined letters from the file, coloured to suit the ground.
