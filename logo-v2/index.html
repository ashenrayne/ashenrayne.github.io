What changed from LogoNew.svg

  • Same drawing, simpler file. The Affinity export nested the shield inside rotated and skewed groups. Every path is now in plain coordinates with the shield's centre line at x = 0, which is what makes the left/right split possible.
  • The windows are now real shapes. In the original, the colour showed wherever the eagle path happened not to cover it, and a clip trimmed the result to the bezel. The clip and the bezel did not quite agree, which is where the border colour bled through. Here the sun and fire areas are their own closed shapes, painted on top of a solid dark shield: rim, shield, eagle panel, windows, each fully covering the one below.
  • The wing joins. Where a wing meets the bezel (the two top corners of the sun and the outer ends of the fire), the corner is replaced by a single curve that leaves the wing and arrives on the bezel without a break in direction. The small joins at the crest and tail feathers get a tight radius so they stay crisp.
  • The tail feathers. In the drawing the two feathers under the body ran on beneath the bezel, so the opening cut them off as blunt, notched stubs that did not match left to right. Crisp now uses one tapered feather each side, mirrored, ending in a point clear of the bezel.
  • The horns. The two dark horns that rose from the back of the head to the top of the shield are gone from Crisp, and from the small cut, to match the open shield's eagle. The sun now runs unbroken over the head. The swept crest feather behind the head stays. The single dark feather that floated in the sun to the left of the neck is gone as well, here and in both small cuts.
  • Small-size cuts. The rim is 5 units wide on a 573-unit drawing, under one pixel below about 112 px, and on black it is the only outline the shield has. The small cut widens the rim to 18 units and the bezel to 20 and closes up every line thinner than about 5 units; the tiny cut uses 30 and 24 and keeps only the main shapes. They are generated from the Crisp eagle by rule, not redrawn by hand, so treat them as a starting point.
  • Open shield. The Shield tab of the first shield page, at the settings saved there: the eagle at 108%, the line 9 units heavier, and a 17.5 unit gap between the line and the wings. The two short stubs of line beside the upper wing tips are left out, and each lower end of the line is cut parallel to the wing over it instead of square. The eagle is the same path, moved into these coordinates; the broken line is flattened into one shape. Each variant gives it one light, radial from the foot of the eagle, and the split variants divide it down the centre line. It has no windows, so the two one-colour variants come out the same, and the Rim switch removes the line. The small-size cuts are still the closed shield.
  • Full eagle, zoomed. The Open shield's eagle, at the same size and position (1.5 times the shield's width), drawn dark over the colour and cropped by the bezel. Nothing is softened or rejoined, so every feather line matches the Open shield. It has one open field instead of two windows, so the fire colour fades in under the sun colour.
  • Diagonal bands. From LogoNewDiagonal.svg: the three bands and their gradient are flattened into the same coordinates and clipped to the colour windows, so they sit behind the eagle in every cut. The band colours follow the Ember, Flame and Sun swatches.
  • Removed. The hidden red fill, the 0.25 px hairline stroke on the eagle, and hairline gaps under one unit wide.
  • Added. Stars in the night sky of the counterchanged variant, and two one-colour versions.
  • Wordmark. Untouched: the outlined letters from the file, coloured to suit the ground.
